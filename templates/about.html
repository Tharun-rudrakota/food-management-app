About FreshPantry

Keep Food Fresh, Cut Waste, Eat Better

FreshPantry was created to solve everyday kitchen challenges: forgotten groceries, unmonitored expiry dates, and unnecessary food waste. By giving you full visibility over your pantry, fridge, and freezer, we help you save money and protect the planet.

Smart Food Inventory

Log and categorize every item in your kitchen. From dairy to fresh produce and dry grains, know exactly what you own and where it's stored.

Proactive Expiry Alerts

Stay one step ahead with intelligent 7-day countdowns. Easily pinpoint which foods need to be consumed immediately before they spoil.

Daily Food Management

Record your daily consumption and meal usage. Plan family dinners around expiring ingredients and celebrate zero-waste kitchen habits.

Food Storage Best Practices

Refrigerator (0°C – 4°C)

Ideal for dairy, opened sauces, leftovers, berries, and leafy vegetables in the crisper drawer.

Freezer (-18°C)

Extend meat, seafood, pre-cooked portions, and frozen fruits by up to 3–6 months with airtight seals.

Dry Pantry (Cool & Dark)

Best for grains, rice, pasta, canned goods, whole potatoes, onions, and cooking oils.
